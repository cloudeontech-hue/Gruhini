Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Delivery areaHyderabad and surrounding serviceable areas
Delivery time24–48 hours from order confirmation
Delivery feeFree on all orders
Cash on DeliveryNot available — all orders are prepaid

1. Order Confirmation

Delivery timelines begin once your payment has been verified and your order has been accepted by the shop owner fulfilling it, not from the moment the order is placed.

2. Delivery Timeframe

Most orders are delivered within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation. As our products are freshly prepared by home-based shop owners, timelines may occasionally vary depending on order volume and product availability.

3. Delivery Area

We currently deliver within Hyderabad and nearby serviceable areas. If you are unsure whether we deliver to your location, please contact us before placing an order.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery is currently free on all orders placed through the Platform.

5. Delayed or Failed Deliveries

If your order is significantly delayed or you believe delivery has failed, please reach out via our Contact Us page and we will help resolve it.
