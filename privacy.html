Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cloudeon Tech Private Limited ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Gruhini Foods mobile application and website (the "Platform").

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name, phone number, and delivery address, provided when you create an account or place an order
  • Order details: items purchased, order value, and order history
  • Payment confirmation details (transaction/payment ID) — we do not collect or store your card, UPI PIN, or banking credentials; these are handled directly by our payment processor, Razorpay
  • Basic technical information (device/browser type) needed to operate the app reliably

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfil your orders, including sharing your name, phone number, and delivery address with the relevant shop owner so they can prepare and deliver your order
  • To verify payments and communicate order status updates
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve the Platform and our product offerings

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Razorpay, a licensed payment aggregator. We do not store your full card or bank account details on our systems. Razorpay's own privacy policy governs how it handles payment data.

4. Data Storage & Security

Order and account data is stored using Supabase (a secure, encrypted database and storage provider) with access controls in place. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share the minimum necessary order/delivery details with the shop owner fulfilling your order, and payment details with Razorpay to process payment. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us — see our Contact Us page.

7. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at children under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out via our Contact Us page.
