1.Introduction

Gruhini Foods ("we," "us," "our") operates a mobile and web application that connects customers with home kitchens and small food businesses for snacks, sweets, and pickles. This policy explains what information we collect from Customers, Shop Owners, and Admins who use the app, why we collect it, and how it is stored and protected.

By creating an account or using Gruhini Foods, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2.Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the app:

Information SharedPurpose
Name and phone numberCreating and identifying your account, so orders can be placed and delivered to the right person

We do not ask for your email address, date of birth, or government ID to use Gruhini Foods as a customer.

3.How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your Customer, Shop Owner, or Admin account.
  • To process and deliver your orders, and keep you updated on their status.
  • To let Shop Owners manage their product catalog and fulfill orders.
  • To let Admins verify payments, manage shop owners, and oversee the marketplace.
  • To auto-fill a delivery address when you choose to share your device location.
  • To maintain the security of accounts and prevent misuse of the app.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

4.Location Data

Gruhini Foods can use your device's GPS location to help fill in your delivery address automatically. This is entirely optional — you can always type your address manually instead.

How it works: when you choose "use my location," your coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service, which converts them into a readable street address. Your coordinates are used only for this conversion and are not stored by Gruhini Foods beyond the resulting address.

5.Payments

All payments in Gruhini Foods are processed through Razorpay, a licensed third-party payment gateway, using UPI, debit/credit cards, or net banking. Card numbers, UPI IDs, and other sensitive payment details are entered directly into Razorpay's secure checkout — Gruhini Foods never receives or stores your raw card or UPI details. We only receive a payment confirmation (a payment ID and status) to verify that your order was paid for.

6.Who We Share Data With

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

7.Data Storage & Security

  • All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • Shop Owner and Admin passwords are hashed with bcrypt before they are stored — we never store passwords in plain text, and your password itself is never visible to us after account creation.
  • Customer account passwords are stored only on your own device and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Our database is protected with Row Level Security (RLS), which restricts what data each part of the app is allowed to read or write.

8.Data Retention

We retain your account and order information for as long as your account remains active, so that your order history and saved details remain available to you. If you delete your account, your customer record is removed as described in the next section.

9.Account Deletion

Customers can delete their account at any time from Profile > Delete Account within the app. This permanently removes your customer record (name, phone number, and address) from our database. This action cannot be undone.

Shop Owners and Admins who wish to delete their account can contact us using the details below.

10.Children's Privacy

Gruhini Foods is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

11.Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: you can view your saved account details from the Profile screen at any time.
  • Correction: you can update your name, address, and other saved details from the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete your customer account and data at any time, as described above.
  • Location: sharing your device location is always optional and can be declined or revoked in your device settings.

12.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected in an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Gruhini Foods after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
