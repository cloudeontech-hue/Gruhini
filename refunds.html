Cancellation & Refunds Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Cancellation windowOnly before the shop owner accepts your order
Refund processing time5–7 business days after approval
Refund methodOriginal payment method, via Razorpay

1. Order Lifecycle

Every order goes through payment verification before being sent to the shop owner for acceptance. Once a shop owner accepts an order, preparation of your fresh, homemade items begins.

2. When You Can Cancel

An order can be cancelled and fully refunded only while it is still in the "Payment Verification" stage — that is, before the shop owner has accepted it. Because our products are freshly prepared to order, we are unable to accept cancellations once a shop owner has accepted and begun preparing your order.

3. Payment Verification Failures

If we are unable to verify your payment, or a shop owner is unable to accept your order (for example, due to stock unavailability), you will be refunded in full automatically.

4. How to Request a Cancellation

To request a cancellation before your order is accepted, contact us immediately via the details on our Contact Us page, quoting your order number.

5. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days and credited back to your original payment method through Razorpay. Actual credit timing to your bank/UPI account may vary depending on your bank.

6. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, incorrect, or missing item, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos of the issue, and we will work with you to resolve it.

7. Contact

For any cancellation or refund request, reach us via our Contact Us page.
